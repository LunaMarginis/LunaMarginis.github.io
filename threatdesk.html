The problem

Defenders face a firehose: advisories, vendor blogs, KEV additions and zero-day reports arrive across dozens of channels. Analysts lose time triaging noise, detection teams struggle to connect a new threat to a rule they can ship, and leaders lack a reliable signal on where the landscape is heading.

The approach

Collect from trusted sources, classify with transparent rules, enrich with exploitation and reputation data, connect to detection content, and only then summarise. Every step is explainable, so an analyst can see why something is ranked where it is.

01

Ingest curated sources

Multiple vetted feeds across government advisories, vendor research and trusted security press. Exploited-vulnerability catalogues, CVE data, exploit-probability scores and the MITRE ATT&CK framework are pulled directly from their primary sources.

02

Classify & score

Deterministic rules tag each item (ransomware, zero-day, APT, supply chain, identity, cloud, AI and more), extract CVEs, and assign critical / high / medium / info severity. Active exploitation lifts an item's rank.

03

Enrich with context

CVEs gain known-exploited status, CVSS severity and exploit-probability scores. Indicators (IP, domain, URL, hash) can be checked across multiple reputation and threat-intel providers, with each provider opt-in.

04

Connect to defence

Threats link to open Sigma, YARA and network detection content, ATT&CK techniques, and a bundled SOC library of incident-response playbooks and tiered runbooks.

05

Brief & decide

An optional LLM analyst drafts summaries, IOC extraction, hunt hypotheses, Sigma/YARA/Snort rules, rule explanations, executive briefs and a daily briefing. Source text is treated as untrusted, and the model is told never to invent IOCs, CVEs or actors.

Features & functionality

Nine capabilities, each answering a question a security team asks daily.

Overview

At-a-glance KPIs: items in the last 24h, critical/high volume, KEV additions over 7 days, new Sigma/YARA rules since last sync, and source health.

Intel Feed

A unified, severity-ranked stream from vendors, CISA advisories and security press, replacing a dozen browser tabs.

Vulnerabilities

Tracks CVEs and Known Exploited Vulnerabilities so patching effort follows real-world exploitation, not just CVSS.

Detections

Surfaces new Sigma and YARA content, closing the gap between a threat headline and a deployable detection.

MITRE ATT&CK

Browse the live Enterprise ATT&CK matrix, with detections and SOC documents mapped to techniques so coverage gaps are visible.

SOC Dept

A searchable SOC knowledge library: incident-response playbooks, tiered runbooks, severity and escalation matrices, and onboarding paths by role.

IOC Lookup

One query across multiple reputation sources for IPs, domains, URLs, hashes and CVEs, with defanged input accepted and an LLM verdict on request.

Sources & Settings

Manage feeds, monitor source health, bring your own API keys, and trigger a full sync, with role-based access.

LLM Daily Briefing

An optional LLM layer condenses the day's intel into a short briefing; it can be switched off for a purely deterministic view.

Leadership lens: foresight, not just feeds

The same data powers strategic decisions, helping managers and leaders see the shape of the threat landscape before it reaches the SOC queue.

  • Spot rising trends early. Trending-topics analytics over 7-day windows show which themes (zero-day, identity, phishing, AI-driven threats, breaches) are accelerating.
  • Understand the evolving landscape. Severity distribution over time separates genuine escalation from background noise and shows whether exposure is growing or shrinking.
  • Prioritise by real-world exploitation. KEV additions and actively exploited flaws give a defensible basis for patching and compensating-control decisions.
  • Measure detection readiness. ATT&CK mapping and new Sigma/YARA content show where coverage exists and where the team needs to invest.
  • Brief stakeholders fast. A concise daily briefing and a ranked "top stories" view turn technical noise into executive-ready talking points.
  • Allocate resources with evidence. Source health and workload signals support staffing, tooling and roadmap conversations with data, not intuition.
  • Lead purple-team priorities. Emerging adversary behaviour feeds directly into emulation plans and detection-engineering backlogs.
